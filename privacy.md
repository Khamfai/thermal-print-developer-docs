# Privacy Policy — M Thermal Print

**Effective date:** 23 November 2026 · [ภาษาไทย](privacy-th.md)

M Thermal Print ("the app") is made by Mounoy Dev. This policy explains what the app does with your information on Android and iOS.

## Summary

- The app does **not** collect, upload, sell or share any personal data.
- There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.
- Everything the app stores stays on your device.

## Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your device only:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Printer settings (name, network address or Bluetooth ID, paper width, printer language) | So you set up each printer only once |
| Print history: the last 20 print jobs, including their content | So you can see what was printed and print it again |
| Apps and websites you allowed to print | So the app asks you only once per app or website |

This data never leaves your device through the app. Deleting a printer or uninstalling the app removes it. On iOS, it can be included in your own device backups, like any other app data.

## Print jobs

Print jobs come from you or from apps and web pages you use (links, share, the Android print menu, or the local print server). The app turns each job into printer commands and sends it **directly to your printer** over your local network or Bluetooth. Jobs are not sent to Mounoy Dev or to any other server.

The optional local print server (Android) listens only on `127.0.0.1`, which means only apps and web pages on the same device can reach it. The first time an app or website asks to print, the app asks for your permission, and you can remove that permission at any time in Settings.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| Bluetooth (Android, iOS) | Find and connect to Bluetooth printers |
| Location (Android 11 and older only) | Android requires it to scan for Bluetooth devices. The app does not read or store your location |
| Local network (iOS) / network access (Android) | Find and print to printers on your Wi-Fi or LAN |
| Notifications (Android) | Show that the local print server is running |

You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your device settings. Only the features that need it will stop working.

## Links to other websites

The About section links to moomou.com and to the developer documentation. These open in your browser, and those websites have their own privacy practices.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
